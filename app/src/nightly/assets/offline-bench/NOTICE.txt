Sample vector tiles for the tile conversion benchmark in Diagnostics.

Each *.sb.mvt.gzip file is one tile exactly as a Shortbread MBTiles extract from
download.geofabrik.de stores it (fetched 2026-11-17 and 2026-11-19), named
<region>-<z>-<x>-<y> in XYZ numbering.
