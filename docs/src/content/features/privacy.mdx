Femto Car Launcher is a home screen, not a service. It requires no account,
contains no advertising and no analytics, and has no server of its own.

## What stays on the device

Calendar and media data are read on the device and stay there. Location
is read on the device and leaves it only inside the requests to the
services you select — the map tiles for the area the map shows, the
weather forecast for your position, and reverse-geocoding queries when
you point the app at your own geocoding host. The trip track log is a
local database; the only way its contents leave the device is a GPX
export you trigger through the system file picker, to a destination you
choose. Saved destinations stay in the app's storage on the device, out
of backup and device transfer; a tap on one hands it to your navigation
app. A video file you pick for the video window is read where it is and
never copied or uploaded; the app keeps only a reference to it, which is
left out of backup and device transfer.

## What goes over the network

Most network traffic goes only to a service you choose to use; the one
exception is the update check, which runs by default but can be turned
off:

- **Map tiles and styles** from the chosen map provider — the keyless
  OpenStreetMap provider by default, or Google Maps once you enter your
  own key.
- **Weather** from MET Norway.
- **Reverse-geocoding queries**, only when you configure a self-hosted
  geocoding host. The default reverse geocoder runs on-device.
- **Fonts** from Google Fonts, only for a family you pick under Settings.
- **Update checks** to GitHub. The automatic check runs at most once a
  day while the app is on, and only while that is turned on under
  Settings → Updates; tapping Check for updates, Update to …, or Retry
  there, or Update on the dashboard's update prompt, checks again right
  away regardless of that setting or the daily limit. No location data
  is sent; the update file itself downloads only when you ask for it.

## Keys you enter

Google Maps keys are entered by you, are sent only to Google, and stay on
the device apart from your own Android settings backup and device
transfer. Google's JavaScript API collects usage data for Google while the
provider is enabled; the [privacy policy](/privacy/) describes that, and
everything else the app does with data, in full.

## Permissions

Notification access, when granted, is used solely to read and control the
active media session for the now-playing card. Location and calendar
permissions each unlock one visible feature; microphone unlocks three —
the voice assistant, dictating a destination in the destination panel,
and the optional music spectrum visualization — and none of them do
anything else. A denied permission renders a reduced card, never a nag.

## Diagnostics and licences

An in-app diagnostics report (**Settings → System → Diagnostics**)
summarises device and runtime facts for troubleshooting. It masks API keys
and is copied only when you tap **Copy report**. The open-source licences
screen lists every bundled third-party component, and the app's own terms
are reachable from **Settings → System → Terms** and on this site under
[Terms](/terms/).
