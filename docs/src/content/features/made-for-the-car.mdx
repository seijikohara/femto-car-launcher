Automotive defaults keep the dashboard legible and operable while driving:
at the default display size, body text stays at or above 16 sp and touch
targets stay at or above 64 dp, and the layout anchors the cards clear of
the driver's view with a left- and right-hand-drive switch. Every default
remains a user choice — this is a general Android app, and the person
holding the device decides.

## Layout

Any orientation and aspect ratio works, from wide head units to portrait
phone mounts; the dashboard reflows across a matrix of screen sizes rather
than targeting one resolution. Display size offers five steps from small to
large. The default (medium) honours the automotive floors above; the small
scale deliberately trades those floors for density, mirroring Android's own
display-size control.

## Three device classes

- **Aftermarket AI boxes** that inject Android into a factory display
  through the car's CarPlay or Android Auto input. These boxes usually lock
  the default-launcher slot, so the launcher starts through the box's own
  boot-up-app hook.
- **Built-in Android head units** — aftermarket units running Android,
  landscape or portrait, at low pixel density and large dp footprints.
- **Smartphones** mounted as a car-navigation display. Here the launcher
  also runs as a regular app; making it the default home screen stays
  optional, because a phone is a shared, everyday device.

## Appearance

Material You dynamic colour by default, fixed accent presets as an
alternative, light / dark / automatic theme, and a configurable glass look
— blur, borders and drop shadows — for the floating cards.

## Typography

The system font by default, any Google Fonts family per slot (a Latin face
plus a Chinese-Japanese-Korean fallback face) downloaded on demand, or any
font already installed on the device — plus user-adjustable text size,
weight and letter spacing. No fonts ship inside the APK and no Play
Services are required.

## Motion, panels and units

- A reduced-motion setting calms animations across the dashboard.
- Calendar, weather and music cards toggle individually; the music card's
  spectrum, album name and cover art each have a visibility switch.
- Speed and temperature units and the clock format follow the user,
  independent of the system locale. No single market is privileged:
  language, units and locale-specific behaviour adapt per device.

## Motion and distraction

The dashboard renders identically regardless of vehicle motion. There is
no driving lockout: distraction responsibility stays with the driver and
the vehicle's own instruments, and the launcher's job is to make the
essentials readable in one glance so that no one has to hunt for them.
