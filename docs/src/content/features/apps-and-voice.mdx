The launcher replaces the home screen, so it also has to be a good way to
reach every other app. It does that with a dock and a full-screen app
panel, both tuned for touch accuracy at automotive distances.

## Dock

An application dock attaches to any screen edge — bottom, top, left or
right — with a compact or extended width. A long-press edits the dock:
buttons and status icons reorder or hide individually, and a reset
restores the default layout.

## App panel

A full-screen application panel offers search, a pinned-apps row, recently
used apps, an A–Z fast-scroll index and three icon-size presets. A
long-press on an app opens App info or uninstalls the app; a long-press on
a pinned app enters an edit mode where pins reorder, drop off the row, or
hide from the panel altogether.

## Voice assistant

A microphone button in the dock captures speech in-launcher with a live
transcript, and falls back to the system voice assistant when no on-device
recognizer exists or the microphone permission is denied. The launcher
brings no assistant of its own: recognition runs through the device's own
speech service, and the request goes to whatever assistant the device
already has.
