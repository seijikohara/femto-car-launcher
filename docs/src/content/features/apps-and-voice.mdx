The launcher replaces the home screen, so it also launches the other installed apps. It does so through a dock and a full-screen app panel, both sized for touch accuracy at automotive distances.

## Dock

An application dock attaches to any screen edge (bottom, top, left or right) with a compact or extended width. A long-press edits the dock: buttons and status icons reorder or hide individually, and a reset restores the default layout.

## Destinations

The dock's navigation button opens a full-screen destination panel. Type or speak a place or an address, then tap **Navigate**: the launcher hands the destination to whichever navigation app the device uses, with no provider built in. A saved place starts navigation to a frequent destination with one tap. Typing, saving and deleting pause while the Global Positioning System (GPS) reads the vehicle as moving; voice and a tap on a saved place work at any time. The panel's header link opens the maps app at the current position.

## App panel

A full-screen application panel offers search, a pinned-apps row, recently used apps, an A–Z fast-scroll index and three icon-size presets. A long-press on an app opens App info or uninstalls the app. A long-press on a pinned app enters an edit mode where pins reorder, leave the row, or hide from the panel.

## Voice assistant

A microphone button in the dock captures speech in the launcher with a live transcript. The launcher falls back to the system voice assistant when no on-device recognizer exists or when you deny the microphone permission. The launcher brings no assistant of its own: recognition runs through the device's own speech service, and the request goes to the assistant the device already has.
