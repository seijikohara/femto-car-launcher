---
title: Glanceable panels
summary: Weather, calendar, now playing, an optional video window, the clock and a status cluster, as floating cards sized for viewing distance. The weather, calendar, now-playing and video cards each open a full-screen panel or player.
order: 3
---

The cards float over the map in a column beside the driver (landscape) or a
band along the bottom (portrait). The cards show large primary figures and
add secondary details only where they help. A tap on the weather or
calendar header opens a full-screen panel with the rest.

## Weather

Current conditions, feels-like temperature, wind and humidity from the
Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway), with an hourly strip on
the card. The full-screen panel is built around a 24-hour temperature
curve, a precipitation nowcast and daily range bars. Forecasts stay
readable offline through an on-device cache.

## Calendar

A multi-day strip plus upcoming events read from the device calendar, with
per-calendar visibility selection and a full-screen agenda panel. Event
colors match the colors of the calendars they come from.

## Now playing

The active media session of any app that publishes one: title, artist,
album, cover art and transport controls, including shuffle and repeat
where the source app supports them. Cover art opens a full-screen player.
Long titles scroll only while the vehicle is parked and truncate while it
moves. A spectrum visualization renders on devices whose audio stack
supports capture.

## Video window

An optional small 16:9 window over the map, on the side away from the
cards, plays a video file you pick from the device or attached USB storage
with the system file picker. A tap opens a full-screen player, which also
picks another file or closes the window. Playing the video pauses other
media, and the audio keeps playing while the launcher is off screen.

The picture stays hidden unless the Global Positioning System (GPS) reads
the vehicle as stopped on a live fix. The picture returns only a few
seconds after the vehicle stops, and the audio keeps playing throughout.
Many places ban video the driver can see while driving, including when
stopped in traffic. GPS cannot tell a parked vehicle from one stopped at a
light, so the window is off by default. Where the screen is out of the
driver's view or local law allows it, you can turn off
**Settings → Panels → Hide the picture while driving**; the app shows a
warning first.

## Clock

A clock that follows the system 12/24-hour preference by default, with
optional seconds. **Settings → Units → Clock** sets a fixed 12-hour or
24-hour format instead.

## Status cluster

Wi-Fi and cellular signal, Bluetooth state, GPS reception and battery level
with charging state, shown in the dock.

## Missing permissions and data

When a permission is denied or a data source is unavailable, the affected
panel renders a reduced state instead of an error screen. You can switch
off the calendar, weather and music cards in **Settings → Panels**, and the
map takes the space they leave. The video window stays off until you
switch it on there.
